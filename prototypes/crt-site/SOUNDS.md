# Prologue sounds

`public/prologue/sfx/` holds short clips cut from CC0 (public domain) recordings on Freesound, heard in
`src/components/Prologue/engine/sfx.js`. Each was trimmed, faded, normalised to about -20 LUFS and saved as 64 kbps
mono MP3 with ffmpeg. No credit is required, but here's where each one came from:
